Crisp

Crisp is a free PDF toolkit in your browser: merge and split PDFs, turn photos into one clean document, reorder, rotate, and export. Free, on your device, no account, no upload. Try it at aykizintelligence.com/crisp.

Help

  • How do I add pages? Drop PDFs or photos onto the box, or click it to choose files. Each PDF is split into its pages; each image becomes one page.
  • How do I merge? Add the files you want, put them in order, and tap Save PDF. Everything becomes one document.
  • How do I split or extract pages? Click the pages you want to select them, then use Save selected to make a new PDF from just those pages.
  • How do I reorder or rotate? Drag a page to a new spot. Use the rotate and remove buttons on each page, or select several and use the Rotate and Delete buttons in the toolbar.
  • How do I make a photo look like a scan? Pick Grayscale or Sharp B&W under Scan look. It cleans up the contrast so a snapshot reads like a proper document.
  • Can I export pages as images? Yes. Export images saves the pages as JPGs (a ZIP when there is more than one).
  • Is there a limit? No daily cap and no file limit. Very large batches depend only on your own device's memory.

The Crisp app
