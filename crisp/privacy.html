Crisp · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Crisp on the web builds your PDF on your device. Your photos and files never leave it.

Crisp (web) is a free tool that turns photos and images into a PDF. It is built so that the documents you work with never leave your browser.

Your photos & files

Everything happens in your browser tab. The images and PDFs you add are read and assembled on your device, and the finished PDF is saved straight to your device. None of it is uploaded to a server, because there is no server to upload to.

Accounts

Crisp has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not know who you are.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare's privacy-first, cookieless web analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint you, and never sees the files you process. It only tells us how many people opened the page.

Advertising & tracking

Crisp contains no ads, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Children

Crisp is a general-purpose utility and is safe for all audiences. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
