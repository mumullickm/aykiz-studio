Yes. Verbatim has no backend to receive your files. The model and your audio live in your browser tab. You can confirm it yourself: open your network tools, transcribe a file, and watch that nothing is sent.
There is no hard limit. Length is bounded only by your own device memory and patience. Very long files take longer because the work happens on your hardware, not a data center.
Plain text (.txt), SubRip subtitles (.srt), and WebVTT (.vtt). You can also copy the transcript straight to your clipboard, with or without timestamps.
It uses OpenAI's Whisper models, which are strong across clear speech and many languages. Choose a larger model for better accuracy, or a smaller one for speed. The transcript is fully editable, so you stay in control of the final text.
It is free, and there is no catch. The honest trade-offs: the model downloads once on first use (a one time wait), and because everything runs locally, very long files are slower than a paid cloud service. For most people, privacy and no limits are worth it.
