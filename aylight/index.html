02 · WHAT IT DOES

The parts you use every day. Nothing more.

Tabs that come back

Quit and reopen, your tabs are where you left them. Closed one by mistake? ⌘⇧T brings it back.

An address bar that remembers

Start typing and it finishes the site you meant from your own history. Anything else goes to Google.

Stay signed in

Your logins live in one private profile on your computer and survive every restart.

Light and dark

Follows your system setting. No theme menu to dig through.

Downloads go to Downloads

No save dialog every time. The file lands in your Downloads folder and aylight shows it to you.

Built on Chromium

Sites work the way they do in Chrome, because the engine underneath is the same.

03 · FIRST OPEN

One honest note before you install.

aylight is new and not yet signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer will ask before it opens it the first time. That is expected.

Mac: drag aylight to Applications, open it once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/aylight.app in Terminal and open it again.

Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux: make the AppImage executable with chmod +x aylight-*.AppImage, then run it.

The full source is on GitHub, so you can read every line before you trust it.
