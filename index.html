Approach

How we work.

Private by design

Data stays on the device wherever the job allows it. Crisp and Verbatim do their work without uploading your files at all.

Reliable on a bad network

We build for the connections people actually have. Core features keep working when the signal does not.

Small on purpose

Fewer features, fewer dependencies and less to break. Most of the work is deciding what to leave out.

Maintained after launch

Shipping is the start. We stay with a product through store reviews, updates, security fixes and growth.

How a project runs
  1. 1
    Scope. We agree on the one outcome that matters, what is out of scope, and how we will measure it.
  2. 2
    Build. A working version on a real device early, then short rounds you can review and test.
  3. 3
    Launch. Store submission, deployment, analytics and security checks, handled as part of the build.
  4. 4
    Operate. Fixes, updates and campaigns after release, for as long as the product needs them.
