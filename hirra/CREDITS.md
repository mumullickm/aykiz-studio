# Credits

## Hero cat mascot
The Hirra hero cat is based on the "Cat face" emoji from **Microsoft Fluent Emoji**,
recolored to the Hirra palette and rigged for cursor tracking, blinking and a happy
expression. Fluent Emoji is released by Microsoft under the **MIT License**, which
permits commercial use and modification.

- Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
- License: MIT (Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation)

## Open Graph card fonts
`assets/og/fonts/` holds the typefaces used to render the share cards
(`assets/og/build_og.py`). They are vendored so the cards can be regenerated
without depending on locally installed fonts. Both families are released under
the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, which permits bundling and commercial use.

- Nunito, Nunito Sans: https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic: https://github.com/IBM/plex
- License: SIL OFL 1.1
