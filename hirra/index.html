The reason

Cats are built to hide illness. By the time it shows, it is often late.

The three most common silent killers, kidney disease, hyperthyroidism and diabetes, all leak the same quiet early signs through the litter box, the water bowl and the scales, long before a cat looks unwell. Hirra is the calm habit that helps you notice.

How it works

Four quiet things, working together.

  1. (01)

    The ten second check-in

    Tap once to log appetite, water, litter, activity and weight. Smart defaults mean a normal day is a single tap.

  2. (02)

    An engine that learns your cat

    Hirra builds a baseline from your cat's own normal, then watches for drift instead of guessing from a generic chart.

  3. (03)

    The pattern vets recognise

    Steady weight loss with rising thirst is the signal clinicians look for. Hirra connects the dots owners usually miss.

  4. (04)

    Private by design

    Your cat's record stays on your device. No account, no tracking, nothing sold. Your data is yours.

Questions

Asked, answered.

What does Hirra mean?

Hirra (هِرّة) is the classical Arabic word for a female cat. Cats are loved in Islamic tradition, and Hirra is built first for cat owners in the Gulf, in Arabic and English.

Does Hirra diagnose my cat?

No. Hirra never diagnoses. It learns your cat's normal, helps you notice changes worth a look, and helps you bring them to a qualified vet. The visit and the decision are always yours.

Is my cat's data private?

Yes. The health record stays on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and nothing is sold. Deleting the app removes its data.

What does the amber mean?

Amber is Hirra's way of saying something is genuinely worth a look, calmly. It is never a red alarm and there is never an upsell on a worrying moment. Most days are quiet.

Do I need Hirra Plus?

No. The daily check-in and the rules-based early-warning engine are free. Plus adds AI vet triage, a weekly digest, a vet-ready PDF and support for more than one cat, for owners who want them.
