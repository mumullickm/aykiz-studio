Support

Help with the calm, private cat health companion.

How does the daily check-in work?

Open Hirra and tap the plus button. Log appetite, water, litter, activity, vomiting and weight. A normal day is a single tap thanks to smart defaults. You can also log hands free with Siri or from the home screen widget.

What is the early warning?

Hirra learns your cat's own normal over time, then watches for drift such as steady weight loss or a sustained rise in thirst, and the weight-plus-thirst pattern that vets recognise. When something is worth attention, Hirra tells you and helps you bring it to your vet.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Hirra never diagnoses. It helps you notice changes and decide whether to see a vet. Always consult a qualified veterinarian.

What is Hirra Plus?

Plus is an optional subscription that adds AI vet triage for a symptom or photo, a weekly health digest, a vet-ready PDF, and support for more than one cat. The daily check-in and the rules-based early-warning engine are free.

Where is my data?

On your device. The free app has no account and no server, and it does not track you. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
