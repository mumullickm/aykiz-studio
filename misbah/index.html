The idea

The more light you carry, the more you can see

Your lamp's oil is your sight into the darkness. Carry a full flame and the world opens wide before you: arches, stars, the path ahead. Let it burn low and the dark closes in. Gather oil to keep the flame alive, and see how far your light will reach.

The play

One finger is the whole game

Hold to rise. Release to fall. That is everything. Drift through a quiet world of pointed arches and eight-point stars, lit gold as your flame passes. There is no rush here, and nothing shouts. Patience over points.

The way

Thread the arches, gather the oil

Ornate arches stand between you and the far dark. Slip through their gaps, catch the drops of oil that hang near them, and your flame answers with more sight. Every step of distance is a step your light was carried.

The soul

When the light goes out, a kindness

A run does not end with a score screen that scolds you. It ends with a breath, a relighting lantern, and a line of reflection from the poets. Then a soft light to begin again.

"Patience is the lantern of the heart." after al-Ghazali
The calendar

Misbah knows what night it is. Through Ramadan the world takes on a warmer plum and a fuller crescent. Through the blessed ten days, it softens toward dawn. On Eid, it greets you.

Questions

Asked, answered

Is Misbah free?

Yes, completely. No price, no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions. Nothing inside it is for sale.

What does Misbah mean?

Misbah (مصباح) is the Arabic word for lamp. The game is about carrying a small light through the dark: your lamp's oil is your sight.

Does Misbah need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline and works in airplane mode. It collects no data, and nothing about your play ever leaves your device.
